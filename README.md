## English

**Effective date and last updated: October 5, 2026**

### 1. Who is responsible and what this policy covers

BOX+ (also identified as BoxMais) is operated by **Max Beckann**, responsible for the personal data processing described here. For privacy questions and requests, contact **booxmais@gmail.com**, with the subject “BOX+ Privacy”.

This policy covers the BOX+ application, its TV interfaces, and the related account, device-linking, playback, support and payment functions operated by BOX+. Available functions may vary by platform and application version. It describes data practices, not prices, media offerings or a grant of rights to third-party material. Independent websites, payment providers, device manufacturers and media services have their own privacy policies for processing under their responsibility.

### 2. Personal data we process

We process information you provide, information generated when you use the application, and relevant confirmations returned by services that support requested functions:

| Category | Examples and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Account and authentication | Name or display name, email, account identifier, authentication credentials and session tokens; used to create accounts, sign in, recover access and protect sessions. |
| Profiles and preferences | Profile name, selected avatar, profile identifiers and available parental-control or playback settings; used to personalize and separate profiles. |
| Playback and application activity | Favorites, watch history, playback position, selected media identifiers and related usage records; used to resume playback and provide account/profile functions. Searches and media selections are processed to return the requested results. |
| Device and connection information | Platform, TV brand/model, operating system, firmware, app version, screen size, supported display capabilities and app-generated session/device-linking identifiers. Internet connections also expose an IP address and request information to the servers contacted. These data support compatibility, account/device management, diagnostics and security. |
| Billing and transactions | Payer name, email, CPF where requested, selected access option, transaction identifiers, amounts, payment status, coupons and billing records; used to process payments and administer access to application features. |
| Support and communications | Messages, account information and attachments you choose to provide when requesting support, and service messages delivered through the application. |
| Advertising and promotions | When enabled, campaign identifier, platform, display event identifier and its association with the account, as well as the relevant advertising preference or eligibility. These records support display scheduling, view counting, duplicate prevention and application of the selected option. |

Do not include unnecessary personal data or third-party confidential information in profile names, support messages or attachments.

In the reviewed Samsung and LG TV implementation, technical device diagnostics do not request the TV's DUID/LGUDID, MAC address or serial number. The current Samsung implementation does not integrate TIFA advertising identifiers. This does not prevent network servers from receiving the IP address needed for a connection. The TV app does not require camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS access for ordinary playback.

### 3. How and why data are used

We use relevant data to provide and maintain account access; link authorized devices; enforce session and access limits; manage profiles; display and play requested media; synchronize favorites and playback progress; process payments; deliver support and operational notices; diagnose failures; prevent fraud and misuse; measure enabled campaign displays; and meet applicable legal obligations or establish, exercise and defend rights.

Under Brazil's LGPD, the applicable basis depends on the operation: performance of a contract or steps requested before entering one for account and application functions; compliance with legal or regulatory obligations for required records; exercise of rights where relevant; legitimate interests, where legally available and balanced against users' rights, for proportionate security and service maintenance; and consent where required for an optional activity. A function being available does not by itself authorize every possible use of its data. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

### 4. Payments

Where a payment function is used, payment information is processed by the provider involved in that transaction. The current TV card-payment integration uses **Mercado Pago**. Card number, expiry and security code entered in that flow are submitted through its SDK to create a payment token; the BOX+ payment request uses that token and related payer/payment information. CPF can also be saved with the account for billing. PIX and transaction confirmations involve the relevant payment infrastructure.

Payment providers may process information for their own fraud prevention, regulatory and transaction-management obligations. Review the provider's policy displayed in the payment flow. This policy does not describe purchases as including media rights or a particular catalogue.

### 5. Advertising and external services

BOX+ can display campaigns or promotions configured through its administration system. Where this function is enabled, the application can record an account-associated display event and retry delivery of that event after a connection failure. We do not describe these events as anonymous. A campaign link or QR code opens the advertiser's or destination service's environment, where that service's policy applies.

The application may request artwork, metadata, trailers or media from external services, including TMDB and YouTube where those features are used. The destination can receive the user's IP address and request details. If a media source or linked service requires credentials, tokens or a playback URL, the information necessary to perform the requested connection is processed by the application and the relevant service. Do not submit credentials for services you are not authorized to access.

### 6. Sharing and recipients

Personal data may be accessed or received, to the extent relevant to their function, by authorized personnel handling operations and support; hosting, database, authentication, storage and network providers; payment processors; media, metadata and image services involved in a request; and communication or support services selected by the user. Campaign delivery can also involve the host of the campaign media or a destination opened by the user.

We may disclose information when required by a lawful request or legal obligation, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend rights. We do not make private account profiles, CPF or authentication credentials public as part of the application. Sharing necessary to operate a requested function is distinct from a provider's separate processing; the latter is governed by its own responsibilities and policy.

### 7. Local storage and shared TVs

BOX+ uses application/browser storage and caches for session state, preferences, catalog data, playback state and, where applicable, queued display events. Authentication and external web flows may also use cookies or equivalent technologies. These mechanisms help maintain sign-in and reduce repeated loading.

You can sign out and use the device's application-data controls to clear local data. Clearing local storage or uninstalling the application **does not automatically delete the server-side account or billing records**. On a shared TV, other people with access to an active session may be able to view profile and activity information. Sign out before transferring or disposing of a device.

### 8. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the category and purpose of the data. Account and profile records are retained as needed to operate the account; activity and preferences support the requested features; transaction and security records may need to remain for applicable obligations, dispute handling and fraud prevention. Temporary cached data and pending events can remain on a device until refreshed, delivered or cleared.

You may request account deletion and deletion of associated personal data using the contact in section 1. We will assess which records can be deleted or anonymized and which must be retained, and explain any applicable exception. Retention in backups and service logs can differ from active databases; those copies remain subject to applicable access and retention requirements. We do not promise that uninstalling, signing out or a deletion request immediately erases all records from every system.

### 9. Security and international processing

Authentication, access restrictions and technical safeguards are used to protect the application and its systems. No transmission or storage method is completely risk-free. Keep passwords and linking codes private and notify the privacy contact if you suspect unauthorized access. Where a security incident requires notification, the applicable notification requirements will be followed.

Depending on the providers involved, data may be processed in Brazil or other countries. Where an international transfer is subject to the LGPD, it must use an applicable lawful transfer mechanism and safeguards. You may request information about the recipients and transfers relevant to your data through the privacy contact. A foreign service being reachable does not mean that every user's data are transferred to every provider.

### 10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and its conditions, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, information about sharing, anonymization/blocking/deletion of unnecessary or unlawfully processed data, portability when applicable, deletion of data processed on consent subject to legal exceptions, information about the consequences of refusing consent, and withdrawal of consent. You may object to processing where legally applicable and request review of a decision based solely on automated processing that affects your interests.

Send requests to **booxmais@gmail.com**. Identify the account and the right you want to exercise. Do not send your password, full card details or security code. We may request proportionate information to confirm identity or authority to act, to avoid disclosing or deleting another person's data. Requests are handled without charge and within applicable legal deadlines. You may also contact Brazil's data protection authority, **ANPD**, or another competent authority.

### 11. Children and adolescents

Profiles and any parental controls do not replace supervision by a parent or guardian or prove a user's age. Where children's or adolescents' data are involved, their best interests and applicable legal requirements must be respected, including parent/guardian involvement or consent when required. A parent or guardian may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of a minor's data, subject to verification of authority.

### 12. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the practices described here change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel where required; additional consent will be requested where the law requires it. Continued use is not treated as blanket consent to unrelated new processing.
